**Full-Stack Developer and Primary Contributor · Four-member team · 2025**

Built ArtNest with a four-member team using Django, Python, HTML, CSS, JavaScript and SQLite. The platform connects artists selling their work with buyers looking to discover, customize and purchase artwork.

My work covered full-stack development across artist and buyer workflows, artwork management, order management and administrative functionality.

The team’s platform includes account and role management, artwork upload and moderation, search and filtering, frame customization with dynamic pricing, shopping cart and checkout, order tracking, payment and refund management, reviews and ratings, likes, messaging, notifications and role-specific dashboards. These describe the overall team project, not a claim that I individually implemented every module.
